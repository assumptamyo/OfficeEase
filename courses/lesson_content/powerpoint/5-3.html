Guided practice · Module 5

Apply this module by editing the Slide Master, adding a custom placeholder to a layout and exporting the result.

Before you start

  • You need: Any presentation with at least three slides.
  • Time: about 15 minutes.
  • Work through the steps in the real application; reading them is not enough.

Steps

  1. Choose View > Slide Master.
  2. Select the top, larger thumbnail (the parent master) and draw a small square accent block in a corner. Check that it appears on the layouts below.
  3. Select the Title and Content layout and choose Insert Placeholder > Picture, then draw the placeholder.
  4. Click Close Master View.
  5. Apply the Title and Content layout to a slide and click the new picture placeholder to add an image.
  6. Choose File > Export > Create PDF/XPS Document and save the presentation as a PDF.

Check your result

Your work is correct if:

  • The accent block appears on every slide and cannot be selected in Normal view.
  • The picture placeholder is available on slides that use the edited layout.
  • The PDF opens with one slide per page.
