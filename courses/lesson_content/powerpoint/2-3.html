Guided practice · Module 2

Apply this module by building a row of three perfectly aligned, evenly spaced shapes and treating them as one object.

Before you start

  • You need: A presentation with a blank slide.
  • Time: about 10 minutes.
  • Work through the steps in the real application; reading them is not enough.

Steps

  1. Draw a rectangle, then hold Ctrl and drag it twice to make three rectangles roughly side by side.
  2. Select all three and choose Shape Format > Align > Align Top.
  3. With them still selected, choose Align > Distribute Horizontally.
  4. Press Ctrl + G to group them.
  5. Change the Shape Fill of the group to dark blue.

Check your result

Your work is correct if:

  • The top edges of the three shapes are level.
  • The gaps between the shapes are equal.
  • Dragging one shape moves all three together.
Go furtherPress Ctrl + Shift + G to ungroup, type a short label in each shape, then group them again.
