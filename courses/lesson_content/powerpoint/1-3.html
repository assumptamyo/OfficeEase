Guided practice · Module 1

Apply this module by building a three-slide presentation, changing its order and adding a speaker note.

Before you start

  • You need: PowerPoint with a blank presentation.
  • Time: about 10 minutes.
  • Work through the steps in the real application; reading them is not enough.

Steps

  1. Create a blank presentation and type a title on Slide 1.
  2. Press Ctrl + M to add Slide 2 and apply the Title and Content layout.
  3. Add Slide 3 and apply the Comparison layout.
  4. Switch to Slide Sorter view and drag Slide 3 so that it becomes Slide 2.
  5. Return to Normal view, select Slide 1 and type this in the Notes pane: Introduce team members and agenda.

Check your result

Your work is correct if:

  • The Comparison slide is now second in the thumbnail pane.
  • The note appears beneath Slide 1 but not on the slide itself.
  • Each slide uses the layout you chose, with its placeholders intact.
