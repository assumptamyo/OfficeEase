Guided practice · Module 3

Apply this module by replacing plain content with visuals: a shaped photograph, a diagram and an icon.

Before you start

  • You need: A presentation and one photograph saved on your computer.
  • Time: about 15 minutes.
  • Work through the steps in the real application; reading them is not enough.

Steps

  1. Insert the photograph on Slide 1 and use Picture Format > Crop > Crop to Shape to make it an oval.
  2. On Slide 2 type a bulleted list of four steps: Research, Design, Build, Test.
  3. Right-click the list and choose Convert to SmartArt, then pick a Process layout.
  4. Choose Insert > Icons, insert one icon and change its Graphics Fill to match your theme colour.

Check your result

Your work is correct if:

  • The photograph is oval and not stretched.
  • The four steps appear as a left-to-right process diagram.
  • The icon is recoloured and stays sharp when enlarged.
