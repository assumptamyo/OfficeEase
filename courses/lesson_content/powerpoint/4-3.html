Guided practice · Module 4

Apply this module by creating a Morph movement between two slides and a timed entrance animation.

Before you start

  • You need: A presentation whose first slide contains one picture and one text box.
  • Time: about 15 minutes.
  • Work through the steps in the real application; reading them is not enough.

Steps

  1. Right-click Slide 1 and choose Duplicate Slide.
  2. On Slide 2, enlarge the picture and move it to the right-hand side.
  3. With Slide 2 selected, choose Transitions > Morph.
  4. On Slide 1, select the text box and apply the Fade entrance animation.
  5. On the Animations tab set Start to After Previous and Delay to 0.5 seconds.
  6. Press F5 to run the show from the beginning.

Check your result

Your work is correct if:

  • The text fades in by itself half a second after the slide appears.
  • Moving to Slide 2 makes the picture glide and grow smoothly rather than jump.
  • Nothing else on the slides moves.
