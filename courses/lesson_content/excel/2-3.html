Guided practice · Module 2

Apply this module by building a small contact list. You will enter data from the keyboard, let Flash Fill complete a pattern and format a column of money.

Before you start

  • You need: A blank workbook.
  • Time: about 10 minutes.
  • Work through the steps in the real application; reading them is not enough.

Steps

  1. In row 1 type the headings First Name, Last Name, Email and Fee, moving across with Tab.
  2. Enter John in A2 and Doe in B2, then add four more people in the rows below.
  3. In C2 type john.doe@company.com.
  4. Click C3 and press Ctrl + E. Flash Fill completes an address for every person.
  5. Type a fee for each person in column D, select the fees and apply the Currency format with 2 decimal places.

Check your result

Your work is correct if:

  • Every row has an email address built from that row's names.
  • The fees show a currency symbol and two decimals, and are aligned to the right.
  • Clicking a fee shows the plain number in the Formula Bar.
