Guided practice · Module 5

Apply this module by writing three decision formulas of increasing difficulty: a simple IF, an IF with AND, and an IFS.

Before you start

  • You need: A blank workbook with sales in column B, years of service in column C and test marks (0 to 100) in column D, starting in row 2.
  • Time: about 15 minutes.
  • Work through the steps in the real application; reading them is not enough.

Steps

  1. In E2 enter =IF(D2>=50, "Pass", "Fail").
  2. In F2 calculate commission: =IF(AND(B2>10000, C2>2), B2*10%, B2*2%).
  3. In G2 assign a letter grade: =IFS(D2>=80, "A", D2>=65, "B", D2>=50, "C", TRUE, "F").
  4. Select E2:G2 and double-click the fill handle to copy all three formulas down the list.

Check your result

Your work is correct if:

  • A mark of exactly 50 shows Pass and grade C.
  • Commission is 10% only where both conditions are met.
  • No cell shows #N/A, because the IFS ends with a TRUE catch-all.
