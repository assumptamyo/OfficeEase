Guided practice · Module 4

Apply this module by turning a list into an Excel Table, summarising it and exploring it with a sort and a slicer.

Before you start

  • You need: A worksheet with about fifty rows of sales data in A3:E50, including Region and Sales Amount columns.
  • Time: about 15 minutes.
  • Work through the steps in the real application; reading them is not enough.

Steps

  1. Click inside the data and press Ctrl + T to convert it into a table.
  2. On the Table Design tab, tick Total Row.
  3. In the Total Row, set column E to Average.
  4. Open Data > Sort. Sort by Region (A to Z), click Add Level, then sort by Sales Amount (Largest to Smallest).
  5. Choose Table Design > Insert Slicer and tick Region.

Check your result

Your work is correct if:

  • The rows are grouped by region, with the largest sale first in each group.
  • Clicking a region in the slicer hides the other regions.
  • The average in the Total Row changes to reflect only the visible rows.
Go furtherAdd a new sale in the row directly beneath the table and confirm that the table expands to include it.
