Guided practice · Module 3

Apply this module by building a small sales sheet that uses a relative formula, an absolute reference and a function together.

Before you start

  • You need: A blank workbook.
  • Time: about 15 minutes.
  • Work through the steps in the real application; reading them is not enough.

Steps

  1. In C1 enter the sales tax rate 0.08 and format it as a percentage.
  2. Enter sales quantities in B3:B10 and unit prices in C3:C10.
  3. In D3 calculate revenue with =B3*C3 and fill the formula down to D10.
  4. In E3 calculate tax with =D3*$C$1, pressing F4 to lock C1, and fill it down to E10.
  5. In E11 enter =SUM(E3:E10) to find the total tax collected.

Check your result

Your work is correct if:

  • D10 reads =B10*C10: the relative references moved.
  • E10 reads =D10*$C$1: the tax rate stayed fixed.
  • Changing C1 to 0.10 updates every tax amount and the total at once.
